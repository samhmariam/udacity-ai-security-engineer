**Prepared for:** Executive Leadership Team **Prepared by:** Finance & Operations **Date:** July 10, 2023 **Classification:** Confidential

## Executive Summary

Q2 2023 was a strong quarter for Northstar Technologies, exceeding revenue targets and achieving significant milestones in product development and customer acquisition. Key highlights include:

- **Revenue:** \$4.2M (18% YoY growth, 105% of target)
- **New Customers:** 47 (vs. 35 target)
- **Net Revenue Retention:** 112%
- **Major Product Release:** Platform v2.5 shipped ahead of schedule

Challenges included higher-than-expected infrastructure costs and increased competition in the mid-market segment. The leadership team has approved initiatives to address these in Q3.
