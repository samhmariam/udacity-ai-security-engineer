WESTFIELD HEALTH
Going Home: General Discharge Guidance and Covered Medications
Audience: Patients and caregivers
Last Updated: February 2026

All names, numbers, and addresses in this training document are fictional. This guide gives general information. Always follow the specific instructions your care team gave you.

---

YOUR FIRST DAYS AT HOME

- Read your After Visit Summary. It lists your medications, activity limits, and follow-up appointments. You can also find it in the MyHealth portal under Visits.
- Take your medications exactly as listed. Don't stop a medication without talking to your care team.
- Keep your follow-up appointment. If one wasn't booked before you left, call Central Scheduling at 555-0100 within 2 business days.

---

WHEN TO GET HELP

Call your care team's nurse line at 555-0120 if you have:
- A fever of 101 degrees F (38.3 degrees C) or higher
- Redness, swelling, or drainage at a wound or incision
- Pain that your medication doesn't control
- Nausea or vomiting that keeps you from taking medications

Call 911 for chest pain, trouble breathing, sudden weakness on one side of the body, or confusion.

---

HOW MEDICATION COVERAGE WORKS

Westfield Health pharmacies accept most insurance plans. Your plan places each medication in a tier:

- Tier 1: Generic medications. Lowest copay.
- Tier 2: Preferred brand-name medications. Medium copay.
- Tier 3: Non-preferred brand-name medications. Highest copay.

Some medications need prior authorization, which means your insurer must approve the medication before it covers it. Your prescriber's office submits the request. Approval usually takes 2 to 5 business days.

To check whether a medication is covered, open the MyHealth portal and choose Pharmacy, then Check Coverage. You can also call the Westfield Health Pharmacy line at 555-0140.

---

FILLING PRESCRIPTIONS

Prescriptions sent at discharge can be picked up at the Main Campus pharmacy (100 Sample Parkway, Garage A level) or mailed to your home at no extra cost. Refills can be requested in the MyHealth portal.
