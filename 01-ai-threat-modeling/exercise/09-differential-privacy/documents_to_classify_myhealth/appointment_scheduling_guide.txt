WESTFIELD HEALTH
Patient Guide: Scheduling and Managing Your Appointments
Audience: Patients and caregivers
Last Updated: January 2026

All names, numbers, and addresses in this training document are fictional.

---

BOOKING AN APPOINTMENT

You can book most appointments in two ways:

1. In the MyHealth portal, choose Appointments, then Schedule a Visit. Pick the department, the visit type, and a time that works for you.
2. Call Central Scheduling at 555-0100, Monday through Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.

Some specialty visits need a referral from your primary care provider first. If a referral is required, the portal tells you before you choose a time.

---

CHANGING OR CANCELING AN APPOINTMENT

Please change or cancel at least 24 hours before your visit. You can do this in the MyHealth portal under Appointments, or by calling Central Scheduling. If you cancel with less than 24 hours' notice three times in 12 months, you may be asked to book future visits by phone.

---

BEFORE YOUR VISIT

- Arrive 15 minutes early for in-person visits.
- Bring a photo ID, your insurance card, and a list of the medications you take.
- Complete eCheck-In in the MyHealth portal up to 3 days before your visit to shorten your wait.
- If you need an interpreter, tell us when you book. Interpreter services are free.

---

VIDEO VISITS

For a video visit, open the MyHealth portal 10 minutes before your appointment time and choose Join Video Visit. Test your camera and microphone ahead of time with the Test My Device button.

---

LOCATIONS AND PARKING

Main Campus: 100 Sample Parkway, Westfield. Free patient parking is in Garage A.
Northside Clinic: 45 Example Road, Westfield. Surface parking is next to the entrance.

---

QUESTIONS

For scheduling questions, call Central Scheduling at 555-0100. For a medical emergency, call 911.
