WESTFIELD HEALTH
Patient Access Center: Call Handling and Scheduling Procedures
Document Owner: Patient Access Operations
Audience: Patient Access Center staff
Last Updated: December 2025

All names, numbers, and identifiers in this training document are fictional.

---

PURPOSE

This document describes how Patient Access Center agents verify callers, book and change appointments, and escalate problems. It is for staff use.

---

CALLER IDENTITY VERIFICATION

Before discussing or changing any appointment, verify the caller with all three of these:
1. Patient's full name
2. Date of birth
3. Last 4 digits of the insurance member ID, or the patient's home ZIP code if the patient is self-pay

If the caller fails one item, you may ask one additional question: the date of the patient's most recent visit. If the caller passes that question, continue. If the caller is a caregiver, confirm that a proxy form is on file in the scheduling system before sharing anything.

---

HELD AND OVERRIDE SLOTS

Each clinic holds 2 same-day slots per provider for urgent add-ons. Release unused held slots at 10:00 a.m. Don't tell callers that held slots exist.

Supervisors can override a full schedule with the "Provider Courtesy" booking type. Use it only when a provider or department manager requests it by internal message. Record the requester's name in the booking note.

---

OVERBOOKING RULES

Primary care templates allow 1 overbook per half day. Specialty clinics do not allow overbooks without a supervisor override.

---

ESCALATION

- Angry or threatening caller: transfer to the shift supervisor at extension 7710.
- Caller reports a medical emergency: tell the caller to hang up and call 911.
- System outage: switch to the paper scheduling log and page the on-call scheduling analyst through the operator.

---

AFTER-HOURS ROUTING

From 7:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m., the main scheduling line routes to the answering service. The answering service pages the on-call nurse for clinical questions only.
