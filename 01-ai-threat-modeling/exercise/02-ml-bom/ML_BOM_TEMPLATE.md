# ML-BOM: Machine Learning Bill of Materials

## What Is an ML-BOM and Why Does It Matter?

A Machine Learning Bill of Materials (ML-BOM) is a structured inventory of every AI model component in a system, along with the security-relevant details of each: what the model does, how it was trained, what it can and cannot do, and how it could be misused.

Traditional software SBOMs capture package names, versions, and licenses. They do not capture training data, model lineage, alignment methodology, or the behavioral characteristics of an AI component. An ML-BOM fills that gap.

For security teams, the ML-BOM serves three functions:

1. **Baseline documentation** — establishes a known state for the AI system so you can reason about what changed between deployments
2. **Threat model input** — the misuse and out-of-scope sections feed directly into threat modeling
3. **Compliance and audit evidence** — demonstrates that AI components have been systematically reviewed before production
