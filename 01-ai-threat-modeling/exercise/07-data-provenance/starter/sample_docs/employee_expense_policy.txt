PRIMARY COMPETITORS

APEX ANALYTICS PLATFORM

List price: $85,000–$220,000 per year depending on seat count and modules. Enterprise deals (500+ seats) have been observed closing at 35–45% discount off list, with multi-year commitments securing an additional 10% reduction. Their standard contract includes annual price escalators of 3–5%.

Win/loss note: We lost two enterprise deals to Apex in Q1 where Apex matched our price and offered a longer free pilot period (90 days vs. our standard 30). Price was not the primary factor in either loss — product fit for compliance reporting workflows was cited.

MERIDIAN DATA SUITE

List price: $60,000–$150,000 per year. Meridian has been aggressive on pricing in the mid-market segment, frequently undercutting by 20% to displace incumbents. Their support tier structure means that the base price often grows significantly with add-ons for dedicated support and advanced analytics.

Win/loss note: We won 4 of 6 competitive deals against Meridian in Q1 where security and compliance certifications were weighted heavily in the evaluation criteria. Meridian does not currently hold FedRAMP authorization, which continues to be a differentiator for us in regulated industries.

VOLTA INTELLIGENCE

Relatively new entrant. Pricing not yet standardized — sales-led and highly negotiated. Observed deal sizes ranging from $40,000 to $95,000 annually. Volta has been offering extended pilots and success-based payment structures to gain reference customers. Not a primary competitive threat at enterprise scale yet, but watch the mid-market.

---

OUR CURRENT POSITIONING

Our list price range of $75,000–$190,000 is competitive for the enterprise segment. However, the mid-market (100–250 seats) is a gap — we are frequently priced above Meridian for deals where buyers do not weight our compliance differentiators. The Q2 pricing strategy discussion should address whether to introduce a mid-market tier or offer a more structured discount framework at that deal size.

---

RECOMMENDED ACTIONS

1. Introduce a mid-market pricing tier for 100–250 seat deals to close the gap with Meridian
2. Extend pilot periods to 60 days as a standard offer (90 days available for strategic accounts with VP approval)
3. Develop a formal competitive battlecard for each of the three primary competitors for use by sales during evaluations
4. Track Volta closely — if they close 3+ enterprise reference customers, their pricing will likely standardize and become a more direct threat
