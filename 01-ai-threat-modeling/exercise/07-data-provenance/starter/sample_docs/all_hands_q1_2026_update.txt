Dear Vertex Consulting team,

We are pleased to share the results of a strong Q1 and to preview the priorities that will guide our Q2 planning cycle. This has been a quarter of meaningful progress across our product, revenue, and people goals, and we want to take a moment to recognize the effort behind those results before turning attention to what comes next.

FINANCIAL RESULTS

Q1 revenue came in at $42.3 million, representing 18% year-over-year growth and exceeding our internal target of $39.5 million. Gross margin improved by 2.1 points compared to Q1 2025, driven by the infrastructure cost optimizations completed in January. We remain on track for our full-year revenue target.

Net new ARR was $8.7 million for the quarter, with strong contributions from enterprise deals in the financial services and healthcare verticals. Customer retention remained above 95%, which continues to be one of our most important leading indicators.

PRODUCT HIGHLIGHTS

The KnowledgeHub internal tool launched in beta to 200 employees in March. Early feedback has been positive, with 87% of beta users reporting that it saved them meaningful time on at least one task in their first week. The team is working through the remaining items on the hardening checklist before a broader rollout.

The platform team shipped three significant reliability improvements in Q1, reducing p99 latency by 34% and bringing our SLA attainment to 99.94% for the quarter. These improvements directly address the feedback we heard during last year's customer advisory sessions.

PEOPLE

We welcomed 23 new team members in Q1 across engineering, sales, and people operations. We also promoted 11 employees to senior or staff-level roles as part of the February promotion cycle. Congratulations to all who were recognized.

Our engagement survey results from February showed overall engagement at 74%, up 6 points from the same period last year. We know there is more work to do, particularly in the areas of cross-functional clarity and career development visibility. Those themes will inform our Q2 people priorities.
