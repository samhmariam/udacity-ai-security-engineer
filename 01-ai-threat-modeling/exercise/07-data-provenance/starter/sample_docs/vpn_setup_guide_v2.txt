VERTEX CONSULTING — IT DOCUMENTATION
VPN Setup Guide
Document ID: IT-DOC-0017
Owner: IT Operations

---

OVERVIEW

This guide describes how to install and configure the Vertex Consulting VPN client on your work device. Follow the steps below to connect to the corporate network from a remote location.

---

INSTALLATION

Step 1: Download the VPN client from the internal software portal at https://sw.vertex-consulting-internal.com/vpn.
Step 2: Run the installer and accept the default settings.
Step 3: Enter your Vertex Consulting email address and your standard SSO credentials when prompted.
Step 4: Complete the MFA challenge.
Step 5: Click Connect.

---

TROUBLESHOOTING

If you cannot connect, verify that your device is running a supported operating system, that your antivirus software is not blocking the VPN client, and that you are not already connected to a conflicting network. Contact the IT help desk at helpdesk@vertex-consulting-internal.com if problems persist.
