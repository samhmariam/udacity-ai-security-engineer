VERTEX CONSULTING — COMPANY ANNOUNCEMENT
Office Location Update: Denver Hub Opening
From: Workplace Experience Team
Date: February 28, 2026

---

We're excited to announce that Vertex Consulting's new Denver hub will open on April 7, 2026.

The Denver hub is located at 1420 Larimer Street, Suite 600, in the heart of downtown Denver. The space accommodates up to 80 employees and features open collaboration areas, private focus rooms, and two large conference rooms equipped for hybrid meetings.

WHO IS THE DENVER HUB FOR?

The Denver hub is open to any Vertex Consulting employee for in-person work. It is the designated primary office for employees hired into Denver-based roles and is available as a secondary workspace for remote employees who travel to the area.

There is no assigned seating. Employees use the workplace app to reserve a desk or conference room before coming in. Instructions for the workplace app are available in the IT help center.

WHAT'S IN THE SPACE?

- 60 open desk stations with monitors, keyboards, and docking stations
- 8 private focus rooms (reservable in 30-minute blocks)
- 2 large conference rooms (capacity: 12 and 20)
- A kitchen stocked with beverages and snacks
- High-speed Wi-Fi on the Vertex Consulting corporate network (use your standard VPN credentials)
- Secure badge access — request access through the IT portal before your first visit

PARKING AND TRANSIT

The office is one block from the 16th Street Mall Free MallRide and a five-minute walk from Union Station. Monthly parking is available at the Larimer Square Garage; contact workplace@vertex-consulting-internal.com to be added to the waitlist.

GETTING BADGE ACCESS

Request badge access through the IT portal at least 48 hours before your first visit. You'll receive a confirmation email when access has been provisioned. Bring your employee ID on your first visit — the front desk can issue a temporary badge if provisioning is delayed.

OPENING WEEK EVENTS

We'll be hosting a casual opening week from April 7–11. Drop by any day between 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM for coffee, a tour, and a chance to meet teammates based in Denver. No RSVP required.

We look forward to welcoming you to the new space.

Questions? Contact the Workplace Experience team at workplace@vertex-consulting-internal.com.

— Workplace Experience Team
